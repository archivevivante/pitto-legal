Help & support — Pitto

Car maintenance & reminders · Published by Archive Vivante inc.

A question, a bug, an idea? Email us, we reply:

pittoapp.support@gmail.com

Good to know

  • Pitto runs 100% on your device — no account, no ads.
  • Switching phones: Settings → Backup exports a file you restore on the new device.
  • Reminders appear as notifications — enable them in Settings → Notifications.
  • Legal info and service intervals are indicative — always check official sources.
